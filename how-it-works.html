From Field to Courtroom

Evidence captured.
Cases built. Justice pursued.

EVIDA is a two-part system — a hardened mobile app for field collection and a powerful desktop platform for analysis — connected by end-to-end encryption and guided at every step by AI.

The Workflow

Four steps from incident to case file.

Every EVIDA investigation follows a structured, secure path — from the moment evidence is captured in the field to the moment a case file is submitted.

1

Capture in the Field

Open the EVIDA mobile app and document what you see — photos, video, audio testimony, written notes. All evidence is geo-tagged, timestamped, and encrypted on-device the instant it's recorded. No internet connection required; everything queues locally until a secure sync window opens.

📍 Geo-tagging 🔒 On-device encryption 📶 Offline-first ⏱ Tamper-proof timestamps
2

Sync Securely to the Desktop

When a secure connection is available, EVIDA syncs your field captures to the desktop platform over an end-to-end encrypted channel. Every file transfer is logged with a cryptographic audit entry — establishing an unbroken chain of custody from the moment of capture.

🔄 Encrypted sync 📋 Chain of custody log 🛡 Zero-knowledge transfer
3

Analyze & Cross-Reference with AI

On the desktop, EVIDA's AI engine processes your evidence — identifying patterns, surfacing connections between incidents, flagging corroborating materials, and building a structured timeline. What would take weeks of manual analysis is surfaced in minutes, without sacrificing accuracy.

🔗 Cross-reference engine 🧠 Pattern detection 📅 Automated timelines 📋 Key findings summaries
4

Export a Submission-Ready Case File

Generate structured, legally defensible case files formatted for ICC submissions, UN reporting bodies, and legal partners. Every export carries a full audit trail and can be shared securely with role-based access controls — so only the right people see the right materials.

⚖️ ICC & UN formats 👥 Role-based access 📤 Secure export 🗂 Full audit trail
The Platform

Two tools. One seamless system.

EVIDA is built as a tightly integrated pair — each tool purpose-built for its environment, designed to work together without friction.

📱 Mobile App 🌍

Built for the Field

The EVIDA mobile app is designed for investigators operating in conflict zones and high-pressure environments. It works without internet, protects data on-device, and can be wiped instantly if the situation demands it.

  • Geo-tagged photos, video, and audio with full metadata preservation
  • Secure offline capture — syncs automatically when connectivity returns
  • Tamper-proof timestamping to establish chain of custody from first capture
  • One-tap emergency data wipe for high-risk situations
  • Witness testimony recording with encrypted transcription
  • Available on iOS and Android
🖥 Desktop Platform ⚖️

Built for the Case Room

The EVIDA desktop platform is where raw evidence becomes a credible case. Import field captures, run AI analysis, collaborate with your team, and generate structured case files ready for legal or international review.

  • Centralized evidence archive with advanced search, filtering, and tagging
  • AI-generated case files, incident summaries, and key findings reports
  • Automatic cross-referencing across incidents, locations, and timeframes
  • Structured exports formatted for ICC, UN bodies, and legal partners
  • Role-based team access with granular permission controls
  • Full cryptographic audit log of every action taken
🔄  End-to-end encrypted sync between mobile and desktop
Security by Design

Encrypted from the
first byte captured.

Evidence handled by EVIDA is encrypted on the device before it ever leaves the field. Data is never stored in plaintext — not during capture, not during transfer, not at rest. Only authorized users with the correct cryptographic keys can access it.

Every action — upload, access, edit, export — is written to an immutable audit log with a cryptographic signature, creating a tamper-proof chain of custody that holds up in international courts.
