Purpose-Built for High-Risk Investigations

Protecting Evidence.
Empowering Justice.

EVIDA is a secure, AI-powered platform designed for those who document the undocumentable — helping investigators capture, protect, and build credible cases from digital evidence in conflict and high-risk environments.

How It Works

Two tools. One seamless system.

EVIDA combines a hardened mobile app for fieldwork with a powerful desktop platform for deep analysis — keeping your entire operation connected and secure.

📱 Mobile App 🌍

Capture in the Field

The EVIDA mobile app is built for investigators operating in conflict zones and high-risk environments. Document incidents, record testimonies, and collect evidence — even offline — with everything encrypted on-device from the moment it's captured.

  • Geo-tagged photos, videos, and audio with metadata preservation
  • Secure offline capture — syncs when connectivity returns
  • Tamper-proof timestamping to establish chain of custody
  • One-tap secure data wipe for emergency situations
  • Available on iOS and Android
🖥 Desktop App ⚖️

Analyze, Organize & Build Cases

The EVIDA desktop platform is where raw evidence becomes a credible case. Import field captures, run AI analysis, cross-reference findings across investigations, and generate structured case files ready for legal or international review.

  • Centralized evidence archive with advanced search and tagging
  • AI-generated case files, summaries, and key findings
  • Automatic cross-referencing of related evidence and incidents
  • Structured exports for ICC, UN bodies, and legal partners
  • Role-based access for secure team collaboration
🔄  End-to-end encrypted sync between mobile and desktop

Security & Confidentiality

The highest standard of protection — for the most sensitive work.

The evidence EVIDA handles can put lives at risk if compromised. That's why every layer of the platform — from how data is captured in the field to how it's stored and shared — is designed with uncompromising security and strict confidentiality at its core.

🔐

End-to-End Encryption

All evidence is encrypted on-device before it ever leaves the field. Only authorized users with the right keys can access it.

🔗

Tamper-Proof Chain of Custody

Every action — upload, edit, access, export — is logged with cryptographic audit trails that hold up in international courts.

👥

Role-Based Access Control

Granular permissions ensure team members only see what they need to. Source identities and sensitive materials stay protected.

🛡️

Built for High-Risk Environments

Secure offline mode, one-tap emergency wipe, and no data stored on third-party servers without your explicit control.
